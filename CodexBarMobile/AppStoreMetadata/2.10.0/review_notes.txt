CodexBar is an iPhone/iPad companion for the Mac app. No separate account login is required. For review without a Mac, dismiss the initial Release Notes with Done, then tap View Demo on the Usage tab while it shows Waiting for Mac Sync to explore synthetic provider usage and costs; the demo Claude detail page shows the new reset credit card. Live data requires the Mac companion on the same iCloud account. Mac download: https://github.com/o1xhack/CodexBar-Mobile/releases . Version 2.10 lists every redeemable usage-limit reset credit (Claude, Codex, Grok) with its exact expiry, and adds an optional Calendar Reminders setting (Settings → Calendar Reminders, off by default). Turning it on asks for calendar Full Access, which is needed to update or remove the events CodexBar creates when a credit is used early; events go into a dedicated calendar named CodexBar and demo data is never written to the calendar. All data comes from the user's own Mac through their private iCloud database; the iPhone makes no provider network requests. The paired Mac version is 0.76.0.2 Mobile 2.10.0.
